CartMate

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CartMate collects, uses, shares, stores, and protects account and device information when you use the CartMate mobile application.

Effective date: August 22, 2026

Who We Are

CartMate is a shopping, cart, store, product, budget, task, account, and expense management app provided by Resolmate.

Contact: cartmate@resolmate.com

Information We Collect

CartMate stores most shopping and expense information locally on your device. CartMate and Resolmate do not store your carts, stores, products, tasks, budgets, accounts, expenses, purchase records, images, or documents on our servers.

Account and Profile Information

  • Name, mobile number, email address, user ID, and login/profile information.
  • Google account access is used only when you choose to connect Google Drive for backup, restore, or upload features.

Device and App Metadata

  • App version, device ID, FCM token, install source, install time, last active time, locale, time zone, and update timestamp.
  • Manufacturer, model, OS type, OS version, network type, battery level, charging status, emulator status, rooted status, available storage value, screen resolution, carrier, and IP address when available.

Shopping and Expense Data Stored Locally

  • Products, services, categories, subcategories, carts, cart items, purchase dates, expiry dates, stores, tasks, budgets, account records, expenses, reports, and transaction history are stored on your device.
  • If you enable Google Drive backup or upload, selected local data, images, documents, and backup files may be saved to your own Google Drive account, not to CartMate or Resolmate servers.

Location Data

  • Approximate and precise location may be used on your device to show nearby stores, nearby products, nearby tasks, maps, and location-based reminders.
  • Background location may be used only for nearby reminders when enabled and permitted by the user.

Photos, Documents, and Files

  • Images captured by camera or selected from the device, including purchase images, receipts, and related documents, are stored locally on your device unless you choose to upload them to your own Google Drive.
  • File names and upload status may be stored locally to show whether an image or document has been uploaded.

Diagnostics, Notifications, and Ads

  • Device and app metadata may be used for app operation, troubleshooting, crash reporting, security, and performance improvement.
  • FCM tokens or notification identifiers may be used to send app notifications where applicable.
  • Advertising identifiers may be used by Google Mobile Ads for advertising and ad performance measurement.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your CartMate profile.
  • To identify your app installation, manage notifications, maintain account state, and support app operation.
  • To save and display carts, products, stores, budgets, accounts, tasks, purchases, and reports locally on your device.
  • To show nearby stores, products, and tasks on your device based on your selected search radius.
  • To send reminders and notifications for carts, tasks, nearby activity, and app features.
  • To back up, restore, and upload selected app data, images, and documents to your Google Drive when you enable that feature.
  • To generate PDFs and reports locally when requested by you.
  • To improve app reliability, diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, and maintain app security.
  • To display ads and measure ad performance through advertising partners such as Google Mobile Ads.

Sharing of Information

CartMate does not sell your personal information. We may share or process information with service providers only as needed to operate app features.

  • Google Firebase may be used for authentication, cloud messaging, app diagnostics, and crash reporting.
  • Google Drive may be used when you choose to back up, restore, or upload app files to your own Google Drive account.
  • Google Maps and location services may be used for maps, nearby features, and store/task location support.
  • Google Mobile Ads may process device and advertising data to display ads and measure ad performance.
  • External apps may be opened when you choose to share store details, PDFs, phone numbers, email addresses, map links, or WhatsApp links.

Permissions Used

  • Location: Used for nearby stores, nearby products, nearby tasks, maps, and location-based reminders.
  • Camera and photos: Used to capture or select purchase images, receipts, and documents.
  • Notifications: Used for cart, task, nearby, and app reminders.
  • Internet: Used for sign-in, Google Drive, maps, ads, diagnostics, and related online features.

Data Storage and Security

CartMate stores your shopping, cart, budget, task, account, and expense records locally on your device. CartMate and Resolmate do not store these records on our servers. If you enable backup or upload features, selected data, images, documents, or backup files may be uploaded to your own Google Drive account.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your Choices and Controls

  • You can control location, camera, photo, and notification permissions from your device settings.
  • You can choose whether to connect Google Drive backup and upload features.
  • You can edit or delete app data inside CartMate where those features are available.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data from your device, subject to your device backup settings.

Language and Translation Accuracy

CartMate may provide app screens, labels, placeholders, notifications, help text, or other content in multiple languages for user convenience. Some translations may be incomplete, incorrect, unclear, or different from the intended meaning in English.

If you notice any incorrect translation or language issue, please let us know by emailing cartmate@resolmate.com. Please include the language, screen name, current text, and suggested correction if possible.

Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your CartMate account, profile information, and device metadata by visiting our account deletion page: https://cartmate.resolmate.com/delete-account.html.

CartMate does not store your carts, stores, products, tasks, budgets, accounts, expenses, purchase records, images, or documents on CartMate or Resolmate servers. To remove local records, delete them in the app or uninstall the app. To remove Google Drive backups, delete those files from your own Google Drive account.

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, accounting, or compliance purposes.

Children's Privacy

CartMate is not intended for children. The app is designed for adult users who manage shopping, budgets, accounts, expenses, and related personal records.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at cartmate@resolmate.com.

This policy should match the data declarations you submit in Google Play Console.
