Delete Your CartMate Account

You can request deletion of your CartMate account and associated app data at any time. This page explains how to submit a request and what data is deleted.

Request Account Deletion

How To Request Deletion

  1. Email us at cartmate@resolmate.com using the subject CartMate Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the phone number and email address used in your CartMate profile so we can identify your account.
  3. We may ask you to confirm ownership before deleting account data.
  4. After verification, we will process your deletion request within a reasonable time period.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Profile information associated with your CartMate account.
  • Stored products, stores, carts, tasks, budgets, accounts, and expenses saved in CartMate.
  • Purchase records, captured images, uploaded documents, and generated reports associated with your account where applicable.
  • Backup records managed by CartMate where we have access and authority to delete them.

Data That May Be Retained

Some data may be retained only when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, accounting, or compliance purposes. We may also retain minimal records showing that a deletion request was completed.

Data stored only on your personal device or in your own Google Drive may also need to be removed by you if it is outside CartMate's control.

Deleting the app from your device does not automatically delete account records or cloud backups. Please submit a deletion request if you want account data removed.

Need Help?

For account deletion questions, contact us at cartmate@resolmate.com.

You can also review our Privacy Policy.
