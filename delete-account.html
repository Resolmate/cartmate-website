Delete Your CartMate Account

You can request deletion of your CartMate account data at any time. This page explains how to submit a request and what account data is deleted from CartMate systems.

Request Account Deletion

How To Request Deletion

  1. Email us at cartmate@resolmate.com using the subject CartMate Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the phone number and email address used in your CartMate profile so we can identify your account.
  3. We may ask you to confirm ownership before deleting account data.
  4. After verification, we will process your deletion request within a reasonable time period.

Data That Will Be Deleted

CartMate does not store your carts, stores, products, tasks, budgets, accounts, expenses, purchase records, images, or documents on CartMate or Resolmate servers. Those records are stored locally on your device or, if you enabled backup, in your own Google Drive.

  • Name, mobile number, and email address associated with your CartMate account.
  • User ID and authentication/profile records associated with your CartMate account.
  • Device and app metadata linked to your account, such as app version, device ID, FCM token, install source, install time, last active time, locale, manufacturer, model, OS type, OS version, network type, charging status, emulator/root status, battery level, storage value, time zone, IP address when available, and update timestamp.

Data Outside CartMate Server Control

Shopping records, store records, product records, cart data, budget data, task data, account records, purchase images, documents, PDFs, and reports are not stored on CartMate or Resolmate servers. To remove local data, delete it inside the app or uninstall the app from your device. To remove backups, delete the backup files from your own Google Drive.

Data That May Be Retained

Some limited records may be retained only when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, accounting, or compliance purposes. We may also retain minimal records showing that a deletion request was completed.

Deleting the app from your device removes local app data from that device, but it does not automatically delete account records from CartMate systems or backup files from your Google Drive.

Need Help?

For account deletion questions, contact us at cartmate@resolmate.com.
